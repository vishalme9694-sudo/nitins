01 / FREE GUIDE

Shuru karne ke liye 4 baatein

Ye woh basics hain jo har naye learner ko pehle samajhne chahiye — koi tip nahi, sirf foundation.

1
Candlestick kya batata hai
Har candle ek time period ke open, high, low aur close price ko dikhati hai — colour se pata chalta hai price upar gaya ya neeche.
2
Trend ko pehchanna
Price hamesha seedhi line mein nahi chalta — higher highs/higher lows uptrend, lower highs/lower lows downtrend batate hain.
3
Risk pehle, profit baad mein
Kisi bhi trade se pehle ye decide karo ki max kitna loss accept karoge — position size usी hisaab se set karo.
4
Ek hi source pe bharosa mat karo
Koi bhi "sure-shot tip" follow karne se pehle khud research karo — market mein guarantee jaisi cheez nahi hoti.
